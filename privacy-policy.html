Privacy Policy: Pipe Puzzle

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Pipe Puzzle ("the game") is made by TwodiStudios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (score, gold, stars, tokens, badges, tower floors, settings and items bought with in-game currency) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device type, app interactions and diagnostic data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This information goes to Google, not to us.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Time check

To stop daily rewards from being collected again by changing the phone's clock, the game reads the current time from a public Google server (a single request to google.com). The request contains no personal information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example "Today's task is waiting!"). They are created on your phone; we do not send them from a server. You can turn them off in Settings > Reminders or in your phone's settings.

Ratings

The game may show Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
